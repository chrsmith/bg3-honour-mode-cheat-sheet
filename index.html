Walkthrough Act I

The early game is the most dangerous part of the run. Leave Act I around level 6. The goal: get the Everburn Blade, recruit everyone, and arrive in the Shadow-Cursed Lands with the end-of-act gear.

Steps are in order. Do them top to bottom; flags mark the moments that can end a run.

Prologue — Nautiloid

  1. 1 Leave the brain pool alone. Keep the intellect devourer 'Us' away from other devourer fights (or neutralise it) — it turns on you if it watches you kill its kin.
  2. 2 Pick up three light containers along the way; you'll stack them later as a barricade.
  3. 3 Grab the Eldritch Rune from the side room (kill the devourers there), use it on the console, free Shadowheart and recruit her.
  4. 4 Press 'Awaken' on the console for an extra XP fight.
  5. 5 Before the transponder room: heal, then stack the three containers in the narrowest doorway to slow the cambions.
  6. 6 Have Shadowheart prepare Command.
  7. 7 Cast Command: Drop on Commander Zhalk to take the Everburn Blade. If it fails, try to kill him. No sword → consider restarting; it's cheap at this point. (encounter card)Potential run killer
  8. 8 Clear the first wave of imps, park a ranged character on the transponder, kill what you safely can, loot, and click the transponder the moment things turn.

Landing Zone

  1. 1 Recruit Shadowheart on the beach. Give the Everburn Blade to Lae'zel once you have her.
  2. 2 Intellect devourers near the beach: only two of you — use the slope and be ready to run. Optional; you can path around to Gale's waypoint.
  3. 3 Kill the unconscious mind flayer without talking to it.
  4. 4 Pull Gale out of the portal and recruit him.
  5. 5 Talk the tieflings into freeing Lae'zel. Use any Inspiration. Killing them breaks your Paladin oath.Potential run killerInspirationOath
  6. 6 Recruit Astarion. Grab the Harper cache by the water.
  7. 7 Fight the goblin raid at the Grove gate now (encounter card).
  8. 8 Enter the Grove and defuse the Zevlor / Aradin standoff.

Emerald Grove

  1. 1 Go past the bear to the sanctum; talk to Arabella's parents, try to talk Kagha down, then report back either way.
  2. 2 Save Nadira from the bugbear assassin.
  3. 3 Be honest with Nettie about your new powers, then take her mind flayer specimen when she leaves the room.
  4. 4 Recruit Wyll. Help the training-dummy tiefling. Side with the tiefling accused of theft.
  5. 5 Speak with Animals (potion or scroll) → talk to the Strange Ox.
  6. 6 Auntie Ethel: play nice, buy all three Elixirs of Hill Giant Strength.
  7. 7 Let Mol's thief 'pickpocket' you — be nice. Send irreplaceable items to camp first; you can reclaim the rest from Mol later.
  8. 8 Pick up Zevlor's quest; bet with Lakrissa; trade with Volo once (first conversation only).
  9. 9 Move the bear off the elevator, deal with the squirrel, try to save (not free) Sazza, help Alfira.
  10. 10 Raise approval with Dammon and Arron by gifting gold (see the cheap-approval trick; re-level with Tav's build). Buy thieves' tools.

Crypt & Chapel Ruins

  1. 1 Use thieves' tools on the locked door by the first waypoint. Disarm or shoot the central trap; leave one character outside.
  2. 2 Loot the skeletons' weapons BEFORE they wake up, grab the book in the side room, then press the button in Withers' chamber and fight.
  3. 3 Recruit Withers. Respec Astarion into Gloom Stalker now.
  4. 4 Clear the tunnel under the Grove near Sazza's cage; rescue the man on the ground.
  5. 5 Loot the small spider nest and the Guidance amulet. Swap it on before important dialogue checks.
  6. 6 Talk peacefully past the goblins (drow dialogue) toward the camp; get the priest of Loviatar buff; free Volo (talk to him before and after the next long rest).
  7. 7 Chapel ruins: approach from the left and pass the paladin check to make the bandits leave. Fail → kill them. Cut the rope, talk your way in, clear and loot.

Blighted Village

  1. 1 Talk to Aradin on the bridge. Recruit Scratch.
  2. 2 Loot the blacksmith's basement (Masterwork Weapon quest) and the apothecary cellar; read the books, kill the skeletons.
  3. 3 Permanently recruit Shovel: temporarily respec someone to Wizard/Warlock, read the scroll outside the mirror (don't learn it), pick the first option on every line, walk through the mirror and talk to Shovel again.Optional cheese
  4. 4 Destroy the Necromancy of Thay. Deceive the ogres into joining you.
  5. 5 Windmill: free Barcus — USE THE BRAKE LEVER. Loot the boots and the smokepowder satchel below.
  6. 6 Kill the sleeping bugbear; try for the knife in the meat.
  7. 7 Kill all goblins in the village, the outpost and the drunk goblins near the mountain-pass path. Then the bloated hyena.
  8. 8 Rescue Mirkon from the harpies (encounter card), then follow him to Mol and loot the Colour Spray ring in the nest.
  9. 9 Clear the swamp traps, kill the mud mephits, take the shadow-druid evidence from the tree. Kill the redcaps; meet the Gur hunter; unlock the waypoint. Expose Kagha later.

Risen Road

  1. 1 Kill the loose gnolls (save Flind for later). Recruit Karlach — your final party is now online.Swap
  2. 2 Bring Wyll to the inn door for the strength check; send only Tav inside. Rescue the trapped man and Florrick, find the dowry, calm the bull.
  3. 3 Zhentarim hideout: send one character in (in case the illithid check fails). Take the shipment quest. Buy Oskar and send him home.
  4. 4 Kill the ogre/hobgoblin couple in the barn. Kill the ogre in Priestess Gut's entrance area and loot the Amulet of Misty Step. Solve the light puzzle.

Underdark

  1. 1 Loot the Selûnite outpost. Bring Shadowheart to destroy the crystal after the minotaur (Inspiration).Swap
  2. 2 Myconid colony: hurt the Bulette as much as possible before it flees. Tell Sovereign Spaw the truth; befriend Derryth, the hobgoblin trader and Omeluum; take every quest; cure Thulla; recruit Glut.
  3. 3 Kill the minotaurs, save Baelen and the Noblestalk; give the Noblestalk to Derryth.
  4. 4 Hook horrors: kill the first, then let the Bulette and the rest fight each other. Flee if it turns.
  5. 5 Get the Sussur bark, forge the Sussur weapon. Loot the dog's grave. Kill the cultists over the dying dwarf.
  6. 6 Back on the surface: kill the owlbear boss, spare the cub (encounter card), the false Paladins of Tyr (bring Karlach), heal Karlach with Infernal Iron, kill Flind and the gnolls, then the Zhentarim — take the shipment chest unopened.

Goblin Camp & Shattered Sanctum

  1. 1 Buy anything good from camp traders first. Clear the camp except Minthara, Dror Ragzlin and Roah. Start with Halsin's prison.
  2. 2 Get branded by Priestess Gut, then fight her in her room before she calls the sanctum (encounter card).
  3. 3 Free the mercenary on the rack and the goblin in the cage. Send the Zhentarim smokepowder barrels to camp.
  4. 4 Hand in the chest at the Zhentarim hideout, buy the Titanstring Bow from Brem, then clear the hideout from the upper level. Loot the Infernal Iron and iron flask (don't open the flask).
  5. 5 Kill both remaining leaders (encounter card); knock Minthara out (non-lethal). Grab the Spider Lyre. Loot Ragzlin's barrels and the Gloves of the Growling Underdog.Potential run killer
  6. 6 Talk to Halsin, Zevlor; party with the tieflings. Heal Padirna's legs.
  7. 7 Glut's spectator ambush, arcane tower (solve Bernard's puzzle; get Omeluum's mushrooms), duergar on the beach. Kill Glut when he asks you to kill Spaw; collect both rewards and the Nere quest. Forge the Mourning Frost staff.
  8. 8 Kuo-toa shrine: Nature check, then claim Blurg's 'god' for yourself. Phase Spider Matriarch (encounter card). Expose Kagha.
  9. 9 Auntie Ethel: find Mayrina's brothers first, start the fight defending Mayrina (encounter card).InspirationOath
  10. 10 Githyanki patrol at the mountain-pass entrance: tell the truth to fight Sarth Baretha (encounter card). Then kill Bernard in the arcane tower.

Grymforge

  1. 1 Show your brand on the boat. Refuse to pay the duergar. Don't long rest until you're ready to free Nere.
  2. 2 Help the archaeologists; team up with the ox (Speak with Animals) to clear rubble; try to lift the invisibility ring from the corpse.
  3. 3 Isolate and kill every duergar except the archaeologists. Steal or persuade for the Runepowder; sell the barrel to the archaeologist trader, kill him, loot it back.Optional cheese
  4. 4 Clear around Nere's rubble, long rest, blow the rubble with your own bombs, stop Nere killing anyone, kill him.
  5. 5 Loot Nere with Tav and roll to decapitate him.Inspiration
  6. 6 Talk to the gnomes (Barcus first). Take Wulbren's quest.
  7. 7 Mimic + Harper cache. Do NOT die to the trapped chest on the ledge on the way.Run killer
  8. 8 Adamantine Forge: animated armours, magma mephits (loot adamantine), catwalk (send a rogue for the pressure plates), merregons, lava elemental. Take the monk's amulet quest; collect moulds.
  9. 9 Grym: the 'Owlbear from the top rope' drop one-shots him (encounter card).Optional cheese
  10. 10 Forge Adamantine Splint Armour (Tav) and Adamantine Scale Mail (Karlach). Loot the Underdark and wilderness — you won't be back.

Mountain Pass & Crèche Y'llek

  1. 1 Death shepherds: kill the second one before it can revive the first. Watch for paralysis.
  2. 2 Send Elminster to camp. Refuse Esther's egg quest but buy The Graceful Cloth for Astarion.
  3. 3 Cable car: walk the path and send ONE person to disarm the repulsor mines — the car can drop the whole party.Run killer
  4. 4 Blue jay quest (Speak with Animals); kobolds; gremishkas; eagles (stay away from the edge); collect all ceremonial weapons; solve the Dawnmaster puzzle for the crest.
  5. 5 Crèche with Lae'zel. Use the trader (only chance). Save the kid in training; stop the infirmary bullies.Swap
  6. 6 Zaithisk chair: respec someone to max INT and WIS first and burn all Inspiration. Don't let Lae'zel go first (DC 30).Inspiration
  7. 7 Clear the Crèche except Kith'rak (gate and hallway guards first). Steal the egg into Lae'zel's pack. Long rest, then fight Kith'rak spread out; disarm her.
  8. 8 Inquisitor: comply and hand over the artefact. When Vlaakith appears, KNEEL.Run killer
  9. 9 Exiting the artefact starts the inquisitor fight — enter turn-based right before you exit to set up.
  10. 10 Grease the left statue before the puzzle. Send ONE character through the trapped hall. Place the Dawnmaster's crest; take the Blood of Lathander without stealing it.Run killer
  11. 11 Confront Voss; remind Lae'zel about the ghustil and to trust Voss. Long rest into Act II.

Act I encounter cards

Threat, how to set up before the fight, what to do on turn one, and how to get out if it goes wrong.

Commander Zhalk (Prologue)
Threat
Very high — he and the cambions will wipe a level-1 party.
Set-up
Barricade the doorway with stacked containers. Shadowheart prepares Command.
Execute
Command: Drop → grab the Everburn Blade. Kill imps, keep a ranged character on the transponder.
Bail-out
Click the transponder. Don't chase XP.
Goblin raid at the Grove gate
Threat
Medium — many goblins, archers on the walls.
Set-up
Arrive from the right side; take the high wall positions near the gate.
Execute
Kill the archers on elevation first. Let the tieflings and druids soak hits. Fire + grease on clumps.
Bail-out
Retreat inside the gate; NPC allies keep fighting.
Harpies (Rescue Mirkon)
Threat
High — the singing harpy charms your party into walking to her.
Set-up
Approach in stealth. The singing harpy always spawns in the same spot; silence that area or pre-target her.
Execute
Kill the singer first. Everyone else focuses the nearest harpy.
Bail-out
Drow Tav has advantage vs charm. Flee if two characters are charmed off cliffs.
Owlbear
Threat
High — Call Consort brings a second owlbear.
Set-up
Fight from the cave-mouth ledge. Pre-buff (Bless, elixirs).
Execute
Open from stealth with Astarion and everything you have. Smokepowder on the mother.
Bail-out
Back out of the cave; owlbears don't chase far.
Phase Spider Matriarch
Threat
High — summons and webs.
Set-up
Break eggs from stealth first. Stay above the drop.
Execute
Kill phase spiders as they blink in; fire on webs.
Bail-out
Climb back up the way you came.
Leaders: Gut, Ragzlin, Minthara
Threat
Very high if they fight together.
Set-up
Fight each leader separately. Pull Ragzlin's followers away or kill them first.
Execute
Gut: kill her in her room before she calls help. Ragzlin: no followers nearby = no Action Surges. Minthara: knock her out.
Bail-out
Doors and chokepoints; leave the building and long rest.
Auntie Ethel
Threat
High — illusions, poison, a fake Mayrina.
Set-up
Find Mayrina's brothers first. Skip the gas traps (feather fall + glorious vaulting or cover vents). See-invisibility (Volo's eye / potion) reveals her.
Execute
Open from an invisibility potion, pre-click to free Mayrina. Splash water on the real Mayrina to tell her apart. The real Ethel's cloak has brown tally marks; illusions are green. Silence stops illusions — drop it before she surrenders.
Bail-out
At ~20 HP stop all damage; get Tav in melee range for the surrender talk; burn Inspiration on Intimidate (breaks oath).
Sarth Baretha (githyanki patrol)
Threat
Very high — melee hits extremely hard.
Set-up
Get a surprise round: send one character in to reposition them if needed.
Execute
Disarm Sarth (Command: Drop from Tav). Focus her; Karlach holds the line.
Bail-out
Be ready to flee and retry.
Grym (Adamantine Forge)
Threat
Very high in a fair fight.
Set-up
One character lowers the platform, then everyone stands above Grym out of combat. Respec someone to Druid (Owlbear form).
Execute
Owlbear + Elixir of the Colossus + Potion of Glorious Vaulting + Longstrider + Enlarge + Feather Fall, then Jump (not Brutal Leap) as close to Grym as allowed.
Bail-out
Missing any buff means no one-shot — use turn-based mode so durations don't tick.

Walkthrough Act II

Act II is short but holds the two biggest run-killers: the Shadow-Cursed Shambling Mound and Ketheric Thorm. Arrive around level 6–7, leave around level 9–10.

Steps are in order. Do them top to bottom; flags mark the moments that can end a run.

Shadow-Cursed Lands

  1. 1 Kill the greeting goblin; at the camp use the illithid check to make the dog-handler kill himself; clear the camp.
  2. 2 If not level 7, do the shadow ambushes now — but NOT Reithwin and NOT the Shambling Mound yet.Potential run killer
  3. 3 Play the Spider Lyre at the goblin camp and kill Kar'niss there (encounter card). Free the pixie (lantern) for the moonlight buff.Boss
  4. 4 Last Light Inn: comply with Jaheira; tell her the parasite is changing you. Don't talk to Isobel yet.
  5. 5 Take Rolan's quest; talk to Mol and Raphael (don't encourage Mol's deal). Bring Karlach to Dammon.
  6. 6 Respec to level 1 and max approval with the Last Light traders (how; re-level with Tav's build). Buy the Cloak of Protection (Talli) and Darkfire Shortbow (Dammon).
  7. 7 Keep feeding Dammon Infernal Iron (Flawed Helldusk pieces). Talk to the Strange Ox, Alfira, the waterfront lady, Florrick; examine Flame Cullagh; report to Halsin.
  8. 8 Fleshmelter Cloak via the morgue; find Arabella and her parents.

Thorm bosses — talk, don't fight

  1. 1 Visit a Thorm boss each time you have 4 Inspiration. The rogue/deception options tend to have the lowest DCs; Tav's Expertise makes this the Bard's best moment.Inspiration
  2. 2 Thisobald: afterwards bring the book to the raven-man; punish the lady but don't make her stab herself (oath). Craft Thisobald's poison.Oath
  3. 3 Malus: kill the staff, keep the Eversight Ring (blindness immunity) for Ketheric. Take the lute back to Halsin and do the portal defence fight.
  4. 4 Gerringothe: loot.

Moonrise Towers

  1. 1 Enter peacefully; choose trial by combat for the goblins; take Z'rell's quest (loyal option).
  2. 2 Destroy the shadow-weave rune in Balthazar's secret room. Turn the gnolls in the kitchen. Talk to Araj with Astarion; drink the strength potion on Tav.
  3. 3 Oubliette hook horror, loot. Kill everyone in the prison and docks; take the tadpoles; free Minthara; collect Wulbren's tools and hand them over.
  4. 4 Escape by boat; get everyone scanned at the Last Light docks; finish Wulbren and Tiefling quests.
  5. 5 Shadowheart's flower; meenlocks + Selûnite resistance; Mason's Guild; meazel; statue shrine (with Shadowheart); acid pit in the morgue.Swap
  6. 6 Cursed kuo-toa: stay on the rock and shoot them out of invisibility for free XP.
  7. 7 Shadow-Cursed Shambling Mound: make sure someone can't be rooted and can run; fight in stages; never near chasms; Arrows of Roaring Thunder carry it (encounter card).Run killer
  8. 8 Clear Moonrise. Bring thunder damage for the eyes; engage the throne room from the main door so you can flee to the waypoint.
  9. 9 READ Ketheric's wife's diary from his chest and keep it.
  10. 10 Oliver fight; githyanki ambush (surprise them).
  11. 11 Isobel: block the side balcony door with a jar first; throw an invisibility potion at her FEET (never target her). Don't bring Shadowheart into the talk. Kill Marcus fast.
  12. 12 Talk to Raphael outside the Mausoleum with Astarion.
⚔ Gear checkpoint: Act II mid-act — after Last Light shopping Open gear table →

Gauntlet of Shar

  1. 1 Shadowheart must be in the party.Swap
  2. 2 Statue puzzle: disarm a vent or Misty Step to the centre.Potential run killer
  3. 3 Cloakers: send only ranged characters to a mushroom they can't reach.
  4. 4 Balthazar: buy his Potion of Speed through the trade window; stash enemy corpses where he can't raise them; lure his guards away; kill him here, not in the Shadowfell (encounter card).Boss
  5. 5 Soft-step trial: walk a character to the end BEFORE activating it. Faith-leap: Misty Step or fly.Optional cheese
  6. 6 Library: shoot the librarian without aggroing the rest. Loot the Spear of Night.
  7. 7 Yurgir: talk him into falling on his sword.Inspiration
  8. 8 Rats: snipe from above; kill the last rat out of combat at range so it never transforms.Optional cheese
  9. 9 Self-same trial: unequip everyone's gear before activating, re-equip after.Optional cheese
  10. 10 Send ONE person down the elevator when placing the orbs.Run killer
  11. 11 DO NOT enter the Shadowfell until you've restocked everything and finished Acts I–II business.

Shadowfell → Moonrise → Colony

  1. 1 Let Shadowheart choose. You do not want the Nightsong fight.Run killer
  2. 2 Return to Moonrise with Jaheira; clear the prison (rest by walking out past the waypoint).
  3. 3 Ketheric at the top: use the dialogue option from his wife's diary.
  4. 4 Mind Flayer Colony: the restoration pod is ONE use in Honour Mode — save it. Kill every devourer, rescue Mizora and Zevlor, kill the Mistress of Souls, do the mind puzzle, purge the waking mind for the permanent buff, take the Resonance Stone.

The Chosen — Ketheric & Myrkul

  1. 1 Never tell Gale to detonate.Run killer
  2. 2 Ketheric & Myrkul: the biggest run-killer. Use everything: elixirs, scrolls, arrows (encounter card).Run killerBoss
  3. 3 Stealth Astarion with the Eversight Ring into a Darkness cloud on a platform; kill the mind flayer first — nothing else can reach him. Other characters wait in stealth at the back.Optional cheese
  4. 4 Free the Nightsong before Ketheric falls (a stealthed, invisible Scratch works).
  5. 5 Myrkul phase: spread out in different directions so only one character is hit at a time; stack blindness / Arrows of Darkness on him; kill necromites and involucres before they heal him.
  6. 6 Loot the Netherstone. Talk to the dream visitor and your allies; long rest; finish anything left before Baldur's Gate.

Act II encounter cards

Threat, how to set up before the fight, what to do on turn one, and how to get out if it goes wrong.

Kar'niss
Threat
High — Fanatic Retaliation punishes whoever kills a fanatic.
Set-up
Use the Spider Lyre to make him come to you alone.
Execute
Focus Kar'niss; if fanatics are present, let non-casters kill them (silence hurts casters most).
Bail-out
Flee toward Last Light.
Shadow-Cursed Shambling Mound
Threat
Run-killer — it swallows and roots.
Set-up
At least one character immune to/away from roots. Arrows of Roaring Thunder.
Execute
Burst from range, retreat, rest, return. Keep away from chasms.
Bail-out
Leave and come back — it does not have to die in one go.
Balthazar (Gauntlet)
Threat
High — raises corpses.
Set-up
Buy his speed potion; hide bodies in camp/chests; bait guards out with Darkness.
Execute
Hard-focus him turn one.
Bail-out
Easier here than in the Shadowfell — commit.
Ketheric & Apostle of Myrkul
Threat
Highest in the game.
Set-up
Diary dialogue, Eversight Ring, Darkness platform, full elixirs, Nightsong freed early.
Execute
Phase 1: kill the mind flayer, then thin the adds. Phase 2: spread out, debuff Myrkul's attacks, kill healers.
Bail-out
Have a Misty Step / Dimension Door escape ready for one character.

Walkthrough Act III

Act III has fewer forced fights; most danger is in Orin and the finale. Reach level 12 before Orin. This route skips most optional dungeons — add House of Hope or Wyrmway only if you want their gear (marked optional in the gear tables).

Steps are in order. Do them top to bottom; flags mark the moments that can end a run.

Wyrm's Lookout

  1. 1 Clear companion dialogue, rest. Misty Step to the portal; snipe the devourers from the roof.
  2. 2 Side with the Emperor and defeat the honour guard (encounter card). If the Emperor dies, the run ends.Potential run killer
  3. 3 Use the astral tadpole; convince everyone else to use one too (respec/swap anyone who refuses).Inspiration

Rivington

  1. 1 Talk to Yenna (be nice). Agree to help the Strange Ox — take the apple.
  2. 2 Kill the mind flayer at the windmill. Side with the refugees at Arfur's mansion; sneak into the basement (Misty Step/fly over traps) for the evidence.
  3. 3 Expose Nestor's trap; get into the circus; have a statue made; buy the Bonespike Garb.
  4. 4 Temple: talk to Yannis; speak with dead on Father Lorgan; tunnels — kill the Bhaalists, take the key and evidence.
  5. 5 Ship fight: side with the Guild against the Stone Lord thugs. Don't send everyone over the steam traps. Loot the tadpole.
  6. 6 Cross the bridge (gnomes, or fly/Misty Step around).

Wyrm's Crossing

  1. 1 Find the Stern librarian body and murder list (Flophouse attic). Hand in to Mamzel and Valeria at Sharess' Caress.
  2. 2 Confront Arfur (no deal). Refuse Raphael's deal via Voss. Harper warehouse fight; speak to Jaheira.
  3. 3 Gortash's audience: only 'consider' his deal for now (accepting breaks your oath).Oath
  4. 4 Talk to Mizora with Wyll; let Ulder Ravengard die when she asks.Swap

Lower City

  1. 1 Take Lora's quest. Donate to the Tabernacle statues (respec to level 1 first — how; re-level with Tav's build).
  2. 2 Dammon: buy Legacy of the Masters. Sorcerous Sundries: pass the deception check with Aradin; buy the Ring of Regeneration.
  3. 3 Solve the Open Hand murders; lie to Sarevok at the tribunal to become an Unholy Assassin; kill Valeria. Buy the Bhaalist Armour.Oath
  4. 4 Restore oath. Undercity Ruins: buy the Bonespike helmet from Bareki; do the grease/fire fight. Exhaust Arabella's dialogue.
  5. 5 Collect tadpoles: Sundries table, Araj's basement, the Lodge, the foundry boat, Flymm Cobblers, Avery (rigged toys).

Why the Paladin dip fixes the level-2 problem

A pure Bard starts with light armour and a d8 hit die — fragile in exactly the fights that keep ending your runs. Starting Tav as a Paladin gives heavy armour (AC 16+ from the first trader set), Lay on Hands, and Divine Smite at level 2. You switch to Bard at level 3 and lose nothing important. Your Bard power — Healing Word, Hold Person, Hypnotic Pattern, Expertise talk-checks — arrives on schedule.

Alternatives if a build doesn't click

Karlach → Path of Giants Barbarian (Patch 8) is a strong, fun swap and uses the same gear. Gale → Abjuration Wizard if you want more defence (Arcane Ward) at the cost of damage. Astarion → Gloom Stalker / Thief (more bonus actions) if you prefer sustained damage over the opening burst.

Why you keep dying at levels 1–2

Read this before you restart. Most early Honour Mode deaths come from a handful of habits, not from the build.

ProblemFix
Fights start with your whole party standing in the open.Enter turn-based mode (F key / toggle) before anyone is spotted. Move each character into position one at a time, then open with Astarion from stealth.
Fighting 'fair' at level 1–2.Most early fights are optional. Skip, talk your way out, or come back a level later. XP from quests and dialogue is just as good.
Standing on flat ground.High ground gives +2 to hit and makes you harder to hit. Ranged characters belong on ledges.
Saving consumables.Throw everything: smokepowder bombs, grease + fire, alchemist's fire, scrolls. Potions are a bonus action — use them.
Waiting too long to run.If two characters are down, flee with whoever can. One survivor keeps the run alive; Withers revives the rest for 200g.
Not resting.Long rest whenever spell slots are low. Camp supplies are cheap; a party wipe is not.
Healing in combat with full actions.Use Healing Word (bonus action) to pick up a downed ally, then keep attacking. Don't spend a full turn topping off HP.

What Honour Mode changes

RuleWhat it means for you
One save file; full party death ends the run.Back up the save folder between sessions. Never let all four fall — flee instead.
Bosses get Legendary Actions.Inspect every boss (right-click → Examine) before the fight. See the Legendary Actions table.
Long rests cost 80 camp supplies (vs 40).Pick up food everywhere; sell junk.
Extra actions from Haste or Elixir of Bloodlust only allow ONE weapon attack.Use the extra action for a potion, a throw, a Dash or a cantrip rather than expecting Extra Attack.
Perilous Stakes (illithid power) only affects allies.Skip it.
Trader prices are higher.Max approval with at least one trader per act — Dammon appears in all three.

Combat fundamentals

TipDetail
Surprise is everything.Attacking from stealth before combat starts surprises enemies — they lose their first turn. You can open dialogue or combat with one character and leave the others frozen out of combat to sneak into position.
Turn-based mode outside combat.It freezes patrols so you can position, throw a potion or set up the Owlbear drop.
Shove and terrain.Shoving an enemy off a ledge is often an instant kill. Grease + fire, water + lightning, and explosive barrels are free damage.
Scratch and Shovel.Scratch (the dog) is a free body that threatens enemies. Shovel's quasit can go invisible indefinitely — useful for triggering surprise.
Cull the Weak.Its damage scales with how many illithid powers you own. Stack powers on one character. The Resonance Stone (Act II) makes it brutal — but it also makes your party vulnerable to psychic damage.
Longstrider every rest.A bench companion can cast it on the whole party after each long rest — more movement makes fleeing easy.

Gold, traders and revives

TipDetail
Withers revives for 200g.Out of combat, use Withers instead of Revivify scrolls. You can pickpocket the gold back from him without penalty.
Cheap max trader approval.Approval cost scales with level. Respec Tav to level 1 at Withers, gift 400g to a trader, then respec back — use Tav's level-up checklist to rebuild.
Traders restock on level-up.Every level-up refreshes stock — buy limited items like Elixirs of Hill Giant Strength again.
Traders drop their valuables.If you plan to kill a trader, sell them your most expensive item first and loot it back.
Camp storage.Send heavy loot, bodies and smokepowder barrels to camp. Sell in bulk.
Hireling inspiration trick.Hire mercenaries with the right background at Withers to earn several Inspiration points from one event; dismiss them after.
Never touch the Oathbreaker Knight.No attacks, no pickpocketing. He fixes broken oaths for gold — don't break your oath casually.
